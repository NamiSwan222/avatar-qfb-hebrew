# Glossary: Avatar: The Last Airbender – Quest for Balance (Hebrew)

Register: friendly, lively Hebrew for a family game, like a good TV dub. Short UI labels. Dialogue sounds
spoken, not literary. Address the player in masculine singular imperative for instructions ("לחץ", "אסוף")
unless the line is a character talking to someone specific (Katara, Toph, Azula... are female: use feminine
forms when they speak about themselves or are addressed).

## Rules for translators

- Keep these tokens exactly: `{0}` `{1}` `{2}`, `<img id="N">`, `<red>`…`</>` (put `<red>` and `</>` around the
  Hebrew word that means the same thing). Keep line breaks where they separate a title from a body.
- Other angle-bracket bits (`<chittering>`, `>ahem<`, `< >`) are spoken sound effects: translate them
  (e.g. `<צקצוק>`, `>אהם<`).
- ALL-CAPS English titles become normal Hebrew (Hebrew has no capitals).
- Numbers stay as digits. Button names (A, B, X, Y, LB, RT, Esc...) stay in Latin letters.
- Write Hebrew in logical order; the game lays it out right-to-left by itself.
- No niqqud, except where a word is ambiguous and very short.
